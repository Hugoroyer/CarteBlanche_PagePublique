Privacy policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

CarteBlanche is published by RoystDev. The app is built to work without an account or a server: your trips stay on your phone.

In short

  • No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • Your countries, regions, cities, trips and photos are stored only on your phone. We have no access to them.
  • The app’s only connection checks whether an update exists. It carries none of your travel data.

What stays on your phone

The countries, regions and cities you tick, your trips, your settings and the copies of the photos you add are stored in the app’s space on your phone. They are part of your device backups (iCloud or computer) according to your own Apple settings. We have no access to your phone or to those backups.

Your photos

The app only accesses the photos you pick yourself. It copies a version into its own space and reads their date and location on the phone, to suggest the matching city and trip. Neither the photos nor their location leave your phone.

Updates

At launch, the app asks Expo’s update service (650 Industries, Inc.) whether a new version exists. That request contains:

  • a random identifier created by the app when it was installed, which does not identify you and is not tied to any account;
  • the installed version of the app;
  • if an update failed to open, a short technical error report.

As with any internet connection, Expo also receives your device’s IP address. Expo stores the random identifier and the IP address with each request, to run the service and count active installations. This information is not used for advertising. See Expo’s privacy policy.

What you choose to share

When you share an image or export a backup, the app creates a file and hands it to your phone’s share sheet. It only goes where you send it. Links to outside websites, such as photo credits, open in your browser and fall under those sites’ rules.

Deleting your data

You can remove a country, city, trip or photo at any time in the app. Deleting the app erases all of its data from your phone. Export a backup first, from Stats then Settings, if you want to keep it.

Children

The app collects no personal data, including from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with its date.
