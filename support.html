Support

A question, a problem, an idea? Write to COURRIEL_ASSISTANCE. Please include your phone model and the app version (Stats, then Settings).

Moving to a new phone without losing anything

In Stats, then Settings, tap “Export my data”. Keep the file it creates (in Files, by email or AirDrop). On the new phone, install CarteBlanche, tap “Import a backup” and pick that file. Your places, trips and photos are restored.

If you restore your new phone from an iCloud or computer backup, your CarteBlanche data comes along too.

Where is my data?

Only on your phone. There is no account and no server. If you delete the app without exporting your data or backing up your phone, it is lost.

My photos get no suggested city

The app suggests a city from the location saved in the photo. Photos without a location, or taken far from any known city, are placed by hand during import.

Does the app work offline?

Yes. The map, city search and all your data work offline.
